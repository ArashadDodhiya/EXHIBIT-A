About the Gallery

Everything here is on display — including you.

Exhibit A started from a simple observation: most custom-print shirt sites look the same. A hero banner, a product carousel, "Best Sellers," a newsletter popup. The clothes become inventory. The person wearing them becomes a customer.

We wanted something different. A place where every piece is treated like a work on display — given a spotlight, a placard, a plate number, a curator's note. Not a star rating and an "Add to Cart" button.

The Wings

The gallery is organized into wings, each with its own mood and visual language.

Wing I — Anime draws from the visual language of manga and anime. Bold lines, halftone dots, stamp-red ink — kinetic energy frozen on cotton.

Wing II — Cinema lives in the moment a great film burns one frame into memory. The light just before the cut, the title card, the reel running out.

Wing III — Commissions is where your words become the artwork. You're commissioning a piece — choosing your line, your type style, your garment — not filling out a text box.

Materials & Print

Every garment is printed on heavyweight cotton — 240gsm for tees, 320gsm for hoodies, 360gsm for crewnecks. We use ring-spun combed cotton for softness without sacrificing structure. The blank itself should feel like something you chose on purpose, not a vehicle for the print.

Prints are screen-printed in small runs. No DTG (direct-to-garment) — screen printing gives a more tactile, opaque, long-lasting result. Designs that require fine detail or metallic finishes use specialty inks applied in multiple passes.
